Who Is Shashwat Yadav?

Shashwat Yadav is a performance marketer and SEO specialist from Raipur. Clients in real estate, healthcare and education work with him to turn ads and search into steady enquiries.

Before agency work, I founded Evento Balloon Decoration and ranked it first organically for “balloon decoration Raipur” without ad spend. Selling, building and marketing a business myself shapes how I plan every campaign.

My own path began with a Mechanical Engineering degree at Shankar Charya Technical Campus (CSVTU), from 2016 to 2020. Sales roles at Byju’s and Lido Learning came next, then Evento from November 2021. A short WordPress and SEO consulting project for MyFemily in early 2026 led into the agency role.

How Shashwat Yadav Approaches Marketing

Most agencies hand the ad, the tracking and the landing page to three different people. I prefer to build the landing page, wire up the tracking and run the ads myself, so nothing gets lost between them.

That habit comes from running a business. At Evento Balloon Decoration I did the selling, the building and the marketing at once, and the business ranked first organically for “balloon decoration Raipur” on Google Business Profile, ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, with zero ad spend.

Today the same thinking guides a 15-person team serving 30+ clients. Every campaign starts from the lead a business actually wants, with tracking wired up so results can be measured.

Leading people is part of the job as well. The work includes managing the team, handling accounts for many clients at once, preparing proposals and pitch decks, and keeping every ad within Google and Meta policy.

How the Campaigns Work

Residential launch

Lead forms asked about budget range, location preference and unit type, so serious buyers were filtered before a single call was made. A parallel brand-awareness campaign showed the project’s amenities. Lead quality was checked against the form answers to refine targeting and creative. The outcome was 516 qualified leads and 128 site visits over five months.

Commercial property

Showroom, office and retail space ran on Meta Ads and Google Ads at the same time, pairing Meta’s audience targeting with Google’s high-intent search. Forms captured preferred location and budget range to filter for serious intent. Cost per lead was tracked closely against a fixed budget.

Hospital services

Each treatment line, from IVF to cataract, painless labour, LASIK and OPD, had its own campaign, so messaging matched each patient’s concerns. Meta Ads used lead forms and click-to-WhatsApp ads, while Google Ads used call tracking to capture answered calls. This describes advertising performance only, not treatment outcomes.
