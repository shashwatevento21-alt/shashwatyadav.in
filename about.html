My Story

From Founder to Performance Marketing Head

I started my career in business development — cold calls and course sales at Byju's, then on-site sales at Lido Learning. In November 2021, I co-founded Evento Balloon Decoration (then Eventoplanner) with two partners. Within a year I was running it solo, and by March 2023 it was incorporated as a private limited company.

I scaled that business to ₹30L in annual revenue with a 4-member core team and 7 gig workers — and I did it without spending a rupee on ads. I ranked #1 organically for "balloon decoration Raipur" across Google Business Profile, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Mode, and built a payment-integrated e-commerce site myself. That hands-on experience — building demand from zero, reading lead quality, living or dying by ROI — taught me the difference between vanity metrics and business outcomes most marketers never learn.

Most agencies hand off the ad, the tracking, and the landing page to three different people. I build the landing page, wire up the tracking, and run the ads myself — so nothing gets lost in translation between them.

In March 2026, I joined The Digital Agency in Raipur as Ad Performance Marketing Head. I lead a 15-person team — 4 social media managers, 4 video editors, a website designer, and 2 graphic designers — across 30+ clients, managing ₹3L+ monthly ad spend on Meta and Google Ads for real estate, healthcare, and education clients.

I build with AI-first tools like Claude Code, so I can move from idea to a shipped, self-running marketing system fast.
