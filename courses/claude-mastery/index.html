Claude and Claude Code Course

Claude and Claude Code, from your first prompt to building websites, tools and automations, taught live in twelve 90-minute classes.

  • 12live classes
  • 90minutes each
  • 18hours in total
  • 4phases
Ask about the next class See upcoming classes

The classes teach how to run campaigns. Results depend on your business, budget, competition and tracking, and are never guaranteed.

Curriculum: Class by Class

Open any class to see what it covers and what to bring. The details below come from the live classes themselves.

Jump to day123456789101112
  1. 1

    Learn what Claude is, what it can and cannot do, how to use the chat, and how to keep your information safe from day one.

    What you will learn

    • What Claude is, and what AI assistants can and cannot do
    • A tour of the Claude chat: conversations, models, files and settings
    • Your first prompts, and how to read the answers critically
    • Why Claude can sound sure and still be wrong, and how to check
    • Privacy basics: what not to paste, and how to protect client data
    • Setting up your account and a simple folder of saved prompts
    What to bringA laptop and a Claude account. Plans and limits change, so check what your plan includes before the class.
  2. 2

    Write clear prompts: give context, a task, examples and a format, then improve the answer step by step.

    What you will learn

    • The parts of a good prompt: role, context, task, constraints and format
    • Showing examples so Claude matches your style
    • Asking follow-up questions and refining an answer in steps
    • Rewriting a weak prompt into a strong one
    • Common mistakes: vague asks, missing context and too many tasks at once
    • Saving the prompts that work as reusable templates
    What to bringA laptop and three real tasks from your work that you would like help with.
  3. 3

    Set up Projects so Claude keeps your context, add files and instructions, and turn repeat work into reusable setups.

    What you will learn

    • What a Project is, and when to use one
    • Adding documents, brand notes and custom instructions
    • Working with uploaded files such as PDFs, documents and sheets
    • Using Artifacts to build and edit documents, pages and small tools in the chat
    • Building a small knowledge hub for one business or client
    • Keeping your Projects organised and clean
    What to bringA laptop and a few documents about a business or client you know, with any private details removed.
  4. 4

    Use Claude to draft ad copy, posts, emails and landing page text in your brand voice, and check every claim before you publish.

    What you will learn

    • Describing your brand voice so the writing sounds like you
    • Drafting ad copy, social posts, emails and landing page text
    • Generating several angles and choosing the strongest
    • Rewriting one message for different platforms and audiences
    • Checking facts, claims and platform policies before publishing
    • Building a reusable content template
    What to bringA laptop and a real offer or product to write about.
  5. 5

    Use Claude to research, summarise and compare information, read reports and sheets, and turn rough notes into clear documents.

    What you will learn

    • Summarising long documents and articles
    • Comparing options and building simple research briefs
    • Reading a report or spreadsheet and asking Claude what it shows
    • Turning notes, calls and ideas into proposals and plans
    • Checking Claude's work: asking for sources, spotting gaps and errors
    • Knowing when a person must make the decision
    What to bringA laptop and a sample report or sheet with private details removed.
  6. 6

    Meet Claude Code: what it is, how to set it up, how it works inside a project folder, and how to give it your first task.

    What you will learn

    • What Claude Code is, and how it differs from chatting with Claude
    • Setting up Claude Code and opening a project folder
    • How it reads, edits and creates files
    • Permissions: what it asks to do and how to stay in control
    • Writing a clear first task and reading what it did
    • Starting, stopping and undoing work safely
    What to bringA laptop. Check before the class which Claude plan or access Claude Code needs, and ask if you are unsure.
  7. 7

    Go from a written brief to a working landing page: plan the page, ask Claude Code to build it, then review and improve it.

    What you will learn

    • Writing a brief: goal, audience, sections and call to action
    • Asking Claude Code to plan before it builds
    • Building a landing page step by step
    • Previewing the page in your browser and spotting problems
    • Improving design, wording and mobile layout with follow-up requests
    • Keeping the page fast, readable and easy to use
    What to bringA laptop and a one-page brief for a real or practice website.
  8. 8

    Review what Claude Code changed, fix problems, keep a safe history of your work, and publish a site without exposing private information.

    What you will learn

    • Reading and understanding the changes Claude Code makes
    • Finding and fixing bugs by describing them clearly
    • Saving your work with Git so you can go back
    • Keeping passwords, keys and private data out of your project
    • Publishing a simple website
    • A checklist to run before anything goes live
    What to bringA laptop and the page you built in Day 7.
  9. 9

    Build a small working tool with Claude Code, such as a lead form or a calculator, and learn how to test it properly.

    What you will learn

    • Choosing a small tool that solves a real problem
    • Planning the screens, the information and the steps
    • Building a form or calculator with Claude Code
    • Saving or sending the information the tool collects
    • Testing with normal and unusual inputs
    • Writing a short note on how the tool works so others can use it
    What to bringA laptop and an idea for a small tool, for example a lead form, a quote calculator or a checklist.
  10. 10

    Learn how automated workflows work in n8n and build a simple one: a form submission that is saved to a sheet and sent on as a notification.

    What you will learn

    • What a workflow is: triggers, steps and actions
    • A first look at n8n and its building blocks
    • Receiving a form submission with a webhook
    • Saving the information to Google Sheets
    • Sending a notification, for example by email or message
    • Testing a workflow and reading what went wrong
    What to bringA laptop and an n8n account or access to an n8n setup. A Google account with Google Sheets is useful.
  11. 11

    Understand what an AI agent is, when to use one instead of a fixed workflow, and how to connect Claude to your tools with clear limits.

    What you will learn

    • What an AI agent is, and how it differs from a fixed workflow
    • Giving an agent a goal, tools and instructions
    • Connecting Claude to everyday tools and data
    • Using Claude Code to help build and edit agent workflows
    • Where agents go wrong: mistakes, cost and privacy
    • Setting limits and keeping a person in the loop
    What to bringA laptop and a repetitive task from your own work that you would like to automate.
  12. 12

    Put it all together: build one end-to-end project, such as a lead page with a form, a sheet and a notification, then review it and plan your next steps.

    What you will learn

    • Choosing a capstone: a page, a tool, a workflow or all three together
    • Building it with Claude and Claude Code
    • Running a review checklist for quality, safety and privacy
    • Presenting your project and getting feedback
    • A recap of the whole course
    • Questions and next steps
    What to bringA laptop and your work from earlier classes.
