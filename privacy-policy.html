Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal information shashwatyadav.in collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to ask for it to be deleted. It describes what the website actually does today.

Who this covers

This website is run by Shashwat Yadav (“I”, “me”). The policy covers the whole site, including the contact form, the chat assistant and Live Classes (sign-in, enrolment and the My Classes page). For anything about your data, write to support@shashwatyadav.in.

What I collect, and why

Live Classes account

  • Name and email address. To create your account, show you your classes and send you emails about them.
  • Google account ID. If you sign in with Google, I also save the ID Google gives me for your account, so the next sign-in finds the same account.
  • Password (older accounts only). Some earlier accounts have a password. It is stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form.
  • Mobile number with country code. Asked when you enrol, so I can reach you about a class if needed. It is checked for format only; it is not verified by SMS. You can edit it on My Classes.
  • Class enrolments. Which class session you joined, when, and its status, together with a copy of your name, email and mobile number as they were when you enrolled.
  • One-time codes. Codes for email verification and password reset are emailed to you. They are stored only as a hash, expire after 10 minutes and can be tried a limited number of times.
  • Emails. I send you an enrolment confirmation (class, date and time, and a link to My Classes) and the codes above. The Meet link is not put in that email; it is only emailed if I have chosen to send calendar invites for a class (see Google Calendar below).

Cookies and sign-in sessions

Live Classes uses only the cookies it needs to work:

  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. The session ends after 2 hours without activity. The server records the session together with your IP address and browser details.
  • An XSRF-TOKEN cookie, a security token that protects forms.
  • A “remember me” cookie if you tick Remember me, and always when you sign in with Google. It keeps you signed in on that browser until you log out.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form I receive what you type: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, the service you need, budget range, your message, and the page you sent it from. It is saved in a Google Sheet that only I use, and an email notification goes to me. You also get an automatic confirmation email.

Chat assistant

The messages you type in the chat window are sent through my server to an AI service (OpenRouter) so it can write a reply; the last few messages of the conversation are included so replies make sense. The site does not keep a log of chats. If you choose to give your name and contact details to the assistant, they are saved as an enquiry in the same Google Sheet as the contact form.

Measurement and hosting

The site uses Google Tag Manager to load measurement tags. Those tags can set their own cookies, and what they measure is configured in Tag Manager rather than in the site’s own code. My hosting provider (Hostinger) also keeps standard server logs.

Google sign-in

When you choose “Continue with Google”, the site asks Google only for your basic profile and email address (your name, email address and account ID). It does not ask for access to your Google Calendar, Drive, contacts or anything else. I do not save your Google profile picture or any Google access token for your account. You can remove the connection at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google Calendar access (site owner only)

Students are never asked to connect a calendar. Only I, as the site owner, connect my own Google account from the admin area, so the site can create, update and delete class events, with Google Meet links, on my calendar.

  • The access requested is Google Calendar event access (calendar.events) plus my email address, to show which account is connected.
  • The site stores the connected account’s email address and a long-lived access token (a refresh token). The token is encrypted in the database and is never shown on any page or sent to a visitor’s browser.
  • I can disconnect at any time from the admin area, which revokes the token at Google and deletes it from the site.
  • By default students are not added to the calendar event. Only if I tick the invite option for a class are the email addresses of its enrolled students sent to Google so Google can email them a calendar invite. That invite contains the Meet link.
  • Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide these class features. My use and transfer of it follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your information

  • Me. In the admin area I can see students’ names, emails, mobile numbers and enrolments, and export them as a spreadsheet for running classes.
  • Other students: no. Students cannot see each other’s details. The class Meet link is visible only to students enrolled in that class (see the Terms).
  • Service providers that handle data for the site: my hosting provider (Hostinger), the email service that delivers the site’s emails, Google (sign-in, Sheets and Gmail for the contact form, Calendar and Meet for classes), OpenRouter (chat replies) and Google Tag Manager.

I do not sell your information and I do not use it for advertising.

How long it is kept

  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Sign-in sessions end after 2 hours without activity (a “remember me” sign-in lasts until you log out).
  • Your Live Classes account, mobile number and enrolments are kept for as long as your account exists, so you can see your class history. I do not currently delete accounts automatically.
  • Contact-form and chat enquiries stay in my Google Sheet until I delete them.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can correct your mobile number yourself on My Classes. To see, correct or delete what I hold about you, email support@shashwatyadav.in from the email address you signed up with and say what you would like. For a deletion request I will delete your account, mobile number and enrolments, and any enquiry you sent, within 30 days, and confirm by email.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords and sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, the Google Calendar token is encrypted, and the admin area is restricted to my account. No online system is perfectly secure, so if you think your account has been misused, tell me right away.

Changes to this policy

If what the site does changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
