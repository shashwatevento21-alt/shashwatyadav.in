A hands-on 3-hour master class. Build a Meta lead generation campaign step by step: audience, placements, ads, instant form and follow-up. Learn what each setting does and how to avoid common beginner mistakes.

What You Will Do

  • 1Creating the lead campaign in Ads Manager
  • 2Audience, placements and Advantage+
  • 3Creative, instant form and follow-up messages
  • 4Reading your first results

What You’ll Learn

Eleven topics, taught in the order you will meet them when you build the campaign.

  1. How a Meta lead generation campaign works and when to use it
  2. Creating the lead campaign in Ads Manager: objective, budget and setup choices
  3. Choosing who sees your ads: location, age and gender, and how much to narrow
  4. Platforms and placements: where your ads appear and when to use Advantage+ placements
  5. Using an existing post or creating a new ad, and the trade-offs of each
  6. Advantage+ audience and the other Advantage+ options, and when to use them
  7. Creative enhancements: what they change in your ad and when to switch them off
  8. Building the instant form: questions, lead quality settings, privacy link and thank-you screen
  9. Following up on leads: message timing, a WhatsApp or call script and a simple lead tracker
  10. Checking lead quality and reading your first results
  11. Policy basics and common reasons lead ads get rejected
What to bringA laptop, a Facebook account and a Page you can use. You build in draft, so no ad spend is needed.
