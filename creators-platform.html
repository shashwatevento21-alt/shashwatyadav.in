About the Platform

A Barter Marketplace for Local Creators

Creators Platform is a directory-style marketplace where creators build a public profile — bio, category, city, portfolio, and exactly what they offer — and get discovered by local businesses looking to trade a product or service for real content.

Every profile goes through an admin review before it's visible in the public directory, so businesses are always browsing genuine, curated creators — not an open, unmoderated list.

This is a separate side project from Shashwat's client marketing work, built and run independently under its own subfolder.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. There's no fee to create a creator profile or to list as a business. The only "cost" is what you agree to exchange directly with the other side — content for a product, service, or credit.

How does the barter exchange work?

A creator lists what they offer (for example, "1 Reel + 3 Stories") and what they're looking for in return (for example, "a free meal for two" or "store credit"). Businesses browse the directory, find a fit, and reach out to agree on the details directly — the platform doesn't process payment or set pricing.

How are creators verified?

Every profile goes through an admin review before it appears in the public directory. This keeps listings genuine and curated rather than open to anyone without review.

What kind of content do creators typically offer?

It varies by creator — commonly Instagram Reels, photo sets, Stories, or written reviews. Each profile states exactly what's included.

Do I need a large following to join as a creator?

No minimum is required. The platform is built around genuine local content, not follower count — what matters is a real profile and a fair exchange.

How do I sign up?

Registration uses a 6-digit one-time code sent to your email — no password link to lose track of. It takes a couple of minutes.
